HOW IT WORKS

  1. Read the question.
  2. Choose your answer.
  3. Your answer will be locked.
  4. Wait for the timer to finish.
  5. Your answer will then be revealed.
  6. Continue to the next question.
  7. Try to achieve your highest score.
